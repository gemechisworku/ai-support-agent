# Complaints and Speaking to a Person

## Speaking to a person
You can ask to speak to a member of staff at any point in a call with the virtual assistant. It will
transfer you to a member of staff with a summary of the call, or, if no one is available, arrange a
callback at a time that suits you.

## Making a complaint
You can complain by phone, in a branch or in writing. Tell us what went wrong and what you would like us
to do. A complaint made on a call with the virtual assistant is recorded and passed to a member of staff.

## Our timelines
- We acknowledge your complaint within 2 business days.
- We aim to resolve it within 15 business days and tell you the outcome in writing.
- If we need longer, we tell you why and when to expect an answer.

## Escalating a complaint
If you are not happy with our answer, ask for your complaint to be reviewed by the complaints officer,
who was not involved in the original decision.

## When the assistant hands over automatically
The virtual assistant passes your call to a member of staff when you ask for a person, raise a dispute
or complaint, describe financial hardship, mention a lawyer or bankruptcy, cannot be verified after two
attempts, or if it repeatedly cannot understand you.
