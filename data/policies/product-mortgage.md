# Home Mortgage — Product Terms

## What the Home Mortgage is
A Meridian Bank Home Mortgage is a long-term loan secured by your home. You repay it in monthly
instalments of principal and interest.

## Rate and term
- Interest rate: variable. The rate is reviewed every 12 months against the bank's reference rate,
  and you are told in writing before any change takes effect.
- APR range: 5.25% to 7.75%.
- Term: 120 to 360 months.

## Grace period and late fee
Your instalment is due on the same day each month. You have a 15-day grace period after the due
date. If the instalment is still not paid in full when the grace period ends, a late fee of 4% of
the overdue principal and interest instalment is added. The fee is charged once per missed instalment.

## Paying early
You can make extra payments or repay the mortgage in full at any time. A prepayment fee of 2% of the
principal repaid early applies during the first 36 months of the loan. After 36 months there is no
prepayment fee.

## Your home and missed payments
Missing mortgage payments can put your home at risk, but no decision about your home is ever made on a
call. If you are struggling, ask about hardship assistance as early as possible.
