# Repayment Schedules and Due Dates

## How instalments work
Every Meridian Bank loan is repaid in monthly instalments. Each instalment is made up of principal
and interest, plus any fees that have been added, such as a late fee. Your repayment schedule lists
every instalment's due date and amount, and it is shown in your loan agreement and on each statement.

## Your due date
Your instalment is due on the same calendar day every month. If that day does not exist in a month
(for example the 31st), the due date is the last day of that month.

## Weekends and public holidays
If a due date falls on a weekend or a public holiday, a payment received on the next business day
counts as paid on time.

## Changing your due date
A due-date change can only be arranged by a member of our staff, and only once in any 12 months. The
virtual assistant can take your request and arrange a callback, but it cannot change the date itself.

## Paying more than the instalment
Any amount you pay above the instalment due is applied to the outstanding principal, which reduces the
interest you pay over the life of the loan. Product-specific prepayment fees are explained in the early
repayment and payoff policy.
