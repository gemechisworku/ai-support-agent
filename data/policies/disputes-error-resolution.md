# Disputes and Error Resolution

## What you can dispute
You can dispute anything you believe is wrong with your loan: an amount owed, a fee, a payment that was
not applied, the interest charged, or something we reported to the credit bureau.

## How to raise a dispute
Tell us by phone, in a branch or in writing. A dispute raised on a call, including with the virtual
assistant, is recorded straight away. You do not need to put it in writing for it to count.

## What happens while a dispute is open
Collection of the disputed amount is paused while we investigate. We will not call you to collect the
disputed amount, and the item is marked as disputed in our credit bureau reporting. Any amount you do not
dispute is still due as normal.

## Investigation and response
A member of staff investigates every dispute. We aim to resolve it and tell you the outcome in writing
within 30 days. If we find an error, we correct your account, refund any fees charged because of it, and
send any needed correction to the credit bureau.

## If you disagree with the outcome
You can ask for the decision to be reviewed through our complaints procedure.
