# Salary-Advance Microloan — Product Terms

## What the Salary Advance is
A Meridian Bank Salary-Advance Microloan is a small, short-term loan for customers whose salary is paid
into a Meridian Bank current account. It is repaid in one to six monthly instalments, usually timed to
your pay date.

## Rate and term
- Interest rate: fixed.
- APR range: 18.00% to 35.99%.
- Term: 1 to 6 months.

## Grace period and late fee
Your instalment is due on the date shown in your agreement. You have a 3-day grace period after the
due date. If the instalment is still not paid in full when the grace period ends, a flat late fee of
$10 is added to that instalment. The fee is charged once per missed instalment.

## Paying early
You can repay a Salary Advance early at any time with no prepayment penalty.

## Borrowing again
While a Salary Advance has a missed instalment, a new advance cannot be opened. Once the account is
up to date you can apply again.
