# Identity Verification and Privacy Procedure

## Right-party check
Before discussing any account, confirm you are speaking to the customer by name. If someone else
answers, such as a family member or colleague, treat them as a third party.

## Verification
After the right-party check, verify the customer with two items: their date of birth and the last 4
digits of the loan number. Both must match the bank's records. No balance, amount, due date, payment or
other account detail may be given before verification succeeds.

## Failed verification
Allow two attempts. After two failed attempts, lock verification for the rest of the call, give no
account information, and offer a callback or a branch visit. The lock is recorded on the call outcome.

## Third parties
A third party gets only a neutral message asking the customer to call the bank back. Do not say why the
bank is calling, do not mention a debt or an amount, and do not confirm that the person has an account.

## What needs no verification
General questions about policies and products, such as how late fees work or whether early repayment is
allowed, can be answered without verification, because they reveal nothing about a customer.

## Privacy
Use customer information only for the purpose of the call. Full account numbers, loan numbers and
national identifiers are never read out; refer to accounts by their last 4 digits. Call recordings and
transcripts are stored with personal details redacted.
