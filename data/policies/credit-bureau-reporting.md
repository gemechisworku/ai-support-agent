# Credit Bureau Reporting

## What we report
Each month we report the status of every loan to the credit bureau: the balance, whether the account is
up to date, and any late payments. Positive, on-time history is reported as well as missed payments.

## When a late payment is reported
A missed payment is reported as late only when the loan reaches 30 days past due. A payment that is late
but made before 30 days past due is not reported as late, although a late fee may still apply.

## Disputed amounts
While you have an open dispute about an amount we report, we mark that item as disputed in our report.

## Correcting an error
If you believe we have reported something incorrectly, raise a dispute. We investigate and, if we made a
mistake, we send a correction to the credit bureau and confirm it to you in writing within 30 days.
