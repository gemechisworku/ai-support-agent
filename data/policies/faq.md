# Frequently Asked Questions

## What do I owe, and when is it due?
Your amount due and due date are on your statement and in the mobile app. The virtual assistant can
tell you your balance, next due date, last payment and loan status once it has verified your identity
with your date of birth and the last 4 digits of your loan number.

## How do I pay?
You can pay in the mobile app, at any branch, or by bank transfer using your loan number as the
reference. Branch payments post immediately; transfers can take up to 2 business days to post. The
virtual assistant cannot take a payment over the phone.

## What happens if I pay late?
After your product's grace period a late fee is added. At 30 days past due the late payment is reported
to the credit bureau, at 60 days past due your account moves to our collections team, and at 90 days
past due the loan may be declared in default. See the missed payments policy for details.

## I can't pay right now. What can I do?
You can make a promise to pay within 14 days, including a partial payment. If your circumstances have
changed, ask about hardship assistance; a hardship specialist will review your situation.

## Can I pay my loan off early?
Yes. Most products have no prepayment fee; mortgages and small-business working capital loans have a
fee in their first years. Ask for a free payoff quote to see the exact amount.

## Can I speak to a person?
Yes, at any time. Ask the virtual assistant and it will transfer you or arrange a callback.
