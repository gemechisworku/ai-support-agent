# Early Repayment and Payoff

## Can I pay my loan off early?
Yes. You can repay any Meridian Bank loan early, either in part or in full, at any time. Paying early
reduces the interest you pay.

## Prepayment fees by product
| Product | Prepayment fee |
|---|---|
| Personal Loan | None |
| Auto Loan | None |
| Home Mortgage | 2% of the principal repaid early during the first 36 months; none after that |
| Small-Business Working Capital Loan | 1% of the principal repaid early during the first 12 months; none after that |
| Salary-Advance Microloan | None |

## Getting a payoff quote
To repay a loan in full, ask for a payoff quote. It shows the exact amount needed on a given date:
the outstanding principal, interest up to that date, any unpaid fees and any prepayment fee. A payoff
quote is free and valid for 10 days. The virtual assistant can give an estimate after verifying your
identity; the formal payoff quote is issued in writing.

## Partial early repayment
An extra payment is applied to the outstanding principal. Your monthly instalment stays the same and
the loan finishes sooner, unless you ask a member of staff to recalculate the instalment instead.

## After the loan is paid off
We confirm in writing that the loan is closed. For an Auto Loan we release our interest in the vehicle,
and for a Home Mortgage we release the mortgage on your home.
