# Small-Business Working Capital Loan — Product Terms

## What the Working Capital Loan is
A Meridian Bank Small-Business Working Capital Loan helps a registered small business cover short-term
costs such as stock, payroll or seasonal gaps. It is repaid in monthly instalments of principal and
interest from the business's account.

## Rate and term
- Interest rate: variable, reviewed every 3 months against the bank's reference rate.
- APR range: 9.50% to 19.50%.
- Term: 6 to 36 months.

## Grace period and late fee
Your instalment is due on the same day each month. You have a 7-day grace period after the due date.
If the instalment is still not paid in full when the grace period ends, a flat late fee of $75 is
added to that instalment. The fee is charged once per missed instalment.

## Paying early
You can repay early, in part or in full. A prepayment fee of 1% of the principal repaid early applies
during the first 12 months of the loan. After 12 months there is no prepayment fee.

## Business changes
Tell us promptly if the business's trading situation changes. A relationship manager can discuss
options; changes to the loan's terms are decided by the bank's business lending team, not on a call.
