# Schedule of Loan Fees

## Late fees
Charged once per missed instalment when it is still unpaid at the end of the product's grace period.

| Product | Late fee |
|---|---|
| Personal Loan | $25 flat |
| Auto Loan | 5% of the overdue instalment, capped at $50 |
| Home Mortgage | 4% of the overdue principal and interest |
| Small-Business Working Capital Loan | $75 flat |
| Salary-Advance Microloan | $10 flat |

## Prepayment fees
| Product | Prepayment fee |
|---|---|
| Home Mortgage | 2% of the principal repaid early during the first 36 months |
| Small-Business Working Capital Loan | 1% of the principal repaid early during the first 12 months |
| Personal Loan, Auto Loan, Salary-Advance Microloan | None |

## Other fees
| Service | Fee |
|---|---|
| Returned or rejected payment | $15 |
| Payoff quote | Free |
| Paper statement | Free |
| Due-date change (once in 12 months) | Free |

## Fees the assistant cannot change
No fee can be waived or refunded by the virtual assistant. Fee disputes are reviewed by a member of
staff under the disputes and error resolution policy.
