# Ways to Pay and Posting Times

## Ways to pay
- **Mobile app:** pay from any Meridian Bank account in the app, under Loans, then Make a payment.
- **Branch:** pay at any Meridian Bank branch counter with cash, a cheque or from your account.
- **Bank transfer:** send a transfer from another bank using your loan number as the payment reference.
The virtual assistant cannot take a payment over the phone. It can explain these options and confirm a
promise to pay.

## Posting times
| Channel | When the payment counts |
|---|---|
| Mobile app | Posted the same business day if made before 8 p.m. local time, otherwise the next business day |
| Branch | Posted immediately |
| Bank transfer | Shows as pending, then posts within 2 business days |

## Pending payments
A payment that has been sent but not yet posted shows as pending. A pending payment received by its due
date counts as on time once it posts. If you have already paid and the payment is pending, you do not
need to pay again or make a promise to pay.

## Paying the right amount
Your statement shows the amount due, including any fees. To pay off the whole loan, ask for a payoff
quote first, because the payoff amount differs from the outstanding balance.
