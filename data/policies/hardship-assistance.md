# Hardship Assistance and Restructuring

## Who can ask for help
Anyone whose ability to pay has been affected by circumstances such as job loss, reduced income,
illness, injury, a death in the family, separation or a natural disaster can ask for hardship
assistance. It is best to ask early, before payments are badly overdue.

## Options that may be available
- A short payment deferral of up to 3 months.
- A temporary reduced payment for an agreed period.
- Restructuring the loan, for example extending the term to lower the monthly instalment.
These options are not automatic. Each one depends on your circumstances and the loan product.

## Who decides
A hardship specialist reviews your situation and decides which option, if any, is offered. The virtual
assistant cannot approve hardship assistance, restructure a loan, change your terms or waive fees. It
records what you tell it and passes your case to a hardship specialist, who will contact you.

## What we may ask for
The specialist may ask about your income and expenses, and for supporting documents. Everything you
share is kept confidential and used only to assess your request.

## While your request is reviewed
Tell us if you can make a partial payment in the meantime. Any amount paid reduces what is overdue.
