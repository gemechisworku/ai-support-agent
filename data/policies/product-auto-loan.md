# Auto Loan — Product Terms

## What the Auto Loan is
A Meridian Bank Auto Loan finances the purchase of a new or used vehicle. The vehicle is the security
for the loan until it is repaid. You repay in equal monthly instalments of principal and interest.

## Rate and term
- Interest rate: fixed for the life of the loan.
- APR range: 5.49% to 14.99%.
- Term: 24 to 72 months.

## Grace period and late fee
Your instalment is due on the same day each month. You have a 10-day grace period after the due
date. If the instalment is still not paid in full when the grace period ends, a late fee of 5% of
the overdue instalment is added, capped at $50 per missed instalment.

## Paying early
You can repay an Auto Loan early, in part or in full, at any time with no prepayment penalty. When the
loan is paid off, we release our interest in the vehicle.

## Insurance and the vehicle
You must keep the vehicle insured for the life of the loan. Missed payments do not change this
requirement. Repossession is never decided on a call; it is only considered after default and is
handled by our collections staff.
