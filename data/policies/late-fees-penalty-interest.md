# Grace Periods, Late Fees and Penalty Interest

## Grace period
Each loan product has a grace period that starts the day after the due date. A payment received in
full within the grace period is not charged a late fee, but the instalment still counts as past due
from the day after its due date.

## Late fees by product
A late fee is added when an instalment is still not paid in full at the end of the grace period. It is
charged once per missed instalment and never more than once for the same instalment.

| Product | Grace period | Late fee |
|---|---|---|
| Personal Loan | 15 days | $25 flat |
| Auto Loan | 10 days | 5% of the overdue instalment, capped at $50 |
| Home Mortgage | 15 days | 4% of the overdue principal and interest |
| Small-Business Working Capital Loan | 7 days | $75 flat |
| Salary-Advance Microloan | 3 days | $10 flat |

## Penalty interest
Meridian Bank does not charge penalty interest on these products. Interest continues to accrue at your
normal contract rate on the outstanding principal, including while a payment is overdue.

## Questions about a fee
If you believe a late fee was charged in error, you can dispute it. Collection of the disputed amount is
paused while we investigate. See the disputes and error resolution policy. The virtual assistant cannot
waive or refund fees; a member of staff reviews every fee dispute.
