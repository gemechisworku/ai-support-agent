# Personal Loan — Product Terms

## What the Personal Loan is
A Meridian Bank Personal Loan is an unsecured loan for personal needs such as home repairs, medical
bills or consolidating other debts. You receive the full amount at the start and repay it in equal
monthly instalments of principal and interest.

## Rate and term
- Interest rate: fixed for the life of the loan.
- APR range: 8.99% to 24.99%, depending on your credit profile.
- Term: 12 to 60 months.
- Your instalment amount and due date are shown on your loan agreement and monthly statement.

## Grace period and late fee
Your instalment is due on the same day each month. You have a 15-day grace period after the due
date. If the instalment is still not paid in full when the grace period ends, a flat late fee of $25
is added to that instalment. The late fee is charged once per missed instalment.

## Paying early
You can repay a Personal Loan early, in part or in full, at any time with no prepayment penalty.
See the early repayment and payoff policy for how to request a payoff quote.

## If you are having trouble paying
Contact us as early as possible. We can explain your options, and a hardship specialist can review
your situation. See the hardship assistance policy.
